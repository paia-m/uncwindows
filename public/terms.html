Service Terms

These terms cover exterior window, gutter and solar panel cleaning offered by Uncles Windows to Auckland homes and businesses. An enquiry is not a confirmed booking.

Quotes and what’s included

Before work starts, check that your quote lists the surfaces or areas to be cleaned, the price and any exclusions. Cleaning does not include repairs unless agreed. If the scope needs to change or extra work is needed, we’ll explain why and agree any price change with you before doing that work.

Access, weather and safety

Please arrange access to the agreed areas and tell us about hazards, fragile surfaces, pets or access restrictions before the visit. If weather, roof conditions or access make work unsafe, we’ll contact you to discuss what can be safely completed or arrange another time.

Changes and payment

If you need to change or cancel a visit, please contact us as soon as you can. Payment timing and any cancellation terms are those agreed for your job before you accept the quote.

If something isn’t right

Call 021 026 33673 with the job details so we can look into it. Nothing in these terms limits rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded under New Zealand law, including the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 and the Fair Trading Act 1986 where applicable.
