Privacy Policy

Uncles Windows provides exterior window, gutter and solar panel cleaning in Auckland. This policy explains how we handle information when you visit our site, ask for a quote or arrange a job.

Information you give us

When you call or otherwise contact us, we may collect your name, contact details, service address and the details you choose to share about the job. We use them to answer your enquiry, prepare a quote, arrange access and carry out the work, and handle billing or follow-up. Providing this information is voluntary, but without enough detail we may not be able to respond, quote or do the work.

Who handles your information

Uncles Windows collects and holds the information you provide. We may share details needed for the job with people helping us deliver it, or with service providers that help us communicate, schedule or invoice. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Website and third-party links

When you send the quote form, Cloudflare hosts the site and Turnstile checks the request for abuse. Resend delivers your name, contact details and message by email to the inbox handling Uncles Windows enquiries. These providers may process data outside New Zealand. We use the information to respond and manage the job, and keep it only as long as needed for those purposes or our legal obligations. The site embeds Google Maps and links to Google reviews. When the map loads or you follow a Google link, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address and process it under Google’s Privacy Policy.

Access, correction and questions

Under New Zealand’s Privacy Act 2020, you can ask for access to personal information we hold about you or ask us to correct it. Call Uncles Windows on 021 026 33673 to make a request or raise a privacy concern. You can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.
